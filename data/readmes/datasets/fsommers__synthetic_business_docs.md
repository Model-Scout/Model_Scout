# A small set of sythentically generated business documents

The documents contain 4 different types of documents: 

 * Apartment lease agreement
 * Personal trainer agreement
 * Medical consent form
 * Nondisclosure form

Each category has 2 different layouts, with mostly (though not completely) identical text between the corresponding layouts.

The documents were generated by prompting GTP-4o to output LaTeX formatted documents of the specified category. The LaTeX output was then processed into PDF.

Most of the documents contain English text, although some also contain Korean text as well. Will be adding more language to this dataset, such as Thai and Burmese.

The documents can be downloaded as follows: 

https://huggingface.co/datasets/fsommers/synthetic_business_docs/resolve/main/data/generated_documents.zip?download=true
