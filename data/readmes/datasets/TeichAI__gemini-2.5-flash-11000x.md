# Gemini 2.5 Flash - 11,000x

This dataset was created by querying Gemini 2.5 Flash over 11,000 times with the goal of compiling the behavior, reasoning traces, output style, and (most importantly) knowledge of the model into a single dataset.

Total Ouput Tokens: 54.4M 

Total Dataset Cost: $134 (USD)

*stats provided via OpenRouter*
