# Korean Price Tags Image Dataset
*This dataset contains high-resolution images of Korean price tags found in retail stores, supermarkets, and markets. It has been curated and anonymized to support AI research in OCR, pricing recognition, product detection, and retail analytics.*

## Supported Tasks

- **Task Categories**:  
  - Image Classification  
  - Text Recognition (OCR)  
  - Product Detection  

- **Supported Tasks**:  
  - Korean text and numeric recognition from price tags  
  - Retail price extraction for product cataloging  
  - Product label and price matching  
  - Multilingual OCR for bilingual price tags (Korean-English)  
  - AI modeling for retail analytics and automated checkout systems  

## Languages

- **Primary Language**: Korean  
- **Secondary Presence**: English (for imported products or international chains)  

## Dataset Creation

### Curation Rationale
This dataset was created to facilitate AI models that interpret retail pricing information from Korean price tags. It supports applications in automated checkout systems, inventory management, retail analytics, and OCR research.

### Source Data
- **Contributors**: Field photographers, retail store visits, and crowdsourced images  
- **Collection Process**: Images were captured from shelves, product displays, and market stands. Sensitive personal data was removed, and no identifiable customers were included in the dataset.  

### Other Known Limitations
- **Bias**: Urban supermarkets and chain stores may be overrepresented compared to smaller markets  
- **Lighting and Clarity**: Some images may include glare, shadows, or reflections  
- **Design Variability**: Different fonts, colors, and layouts may affect OCR accuracy  

## Intended Uses

### ✅ Direct Use
- Training OCR models for price and product recognition  
- Retail analytics and automated inventory systems  
- Visual-language model training for e-commerce and shopping assistants  
- Research in computer vision and text detection in real-world retail environments  

### ❌ Out-of-Scope Use
- Tracking or profiling individuals in stores  
- Commercial reuse of proprietary price tag designs without consent  
- Misuse of retail data for unauthorized marketing or competitor analysis
