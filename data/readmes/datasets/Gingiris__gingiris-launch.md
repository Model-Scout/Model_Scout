### What Is This?

The **Gingiris AI Product Global Launch Playbook** is an operational guide covering the full arc of a product launch: pre-launch positioning, Product Hunt day execution, KOL/influencer outreach, Reddit and community seeding, UGC growth loops, and post-launch momentum maintenance.

Unlike generic marketing templates, this playbook is built from **real launch campaigns** — Manus (AI agent), Devin (first AI software engineer), AFFiNE (open-source Notion alternative), and 27+ other AI products that hit Product Hunt #1. Every tactic has a specific time, template, and decision point.

### Field-Tested Benchmarks (2024–2026, 105+ projects)

Fresh numbers from real launch campaigns run between 2024 and mid-2026:

| Benchmark | Value |
|:---|:---|
| PH vote pacing ceiling (avoid vote sweeps) | ≤100 votes/hour total, ≤30/hour per channel |
| Weekly vs. daily badge exposure | ~7× daily; monthly badge ~30× |
| PH exposure → upvote conversion | ~1% (vs. Reddit open-source promo → star at 5–8%) |
| Hacker News front-page bar | 30 upvotes + 10 comments within 30 minutes |
| $1K launch recipe | a few hundred signups (micro YouTube ads + niche subreddits + topic communities) |
| $10–20K campaign | ~1M impressions, low-thousands signups |
| Measured launch-day CAC (backward-planned traffic, full UTM attribution) | $6.25 per signup on a 500K-visit day |

Rule of thumb that surprises most founders: **on a small budget, skip Product Hunt** — the same spend on ~10 Twitter KOL/KOCs delivers more exposure and more signups.

### What's New (July 2026): Zero-Budget Tactics from Founder Interviews

Freshly added tactics distilled from a new round of founder interviews — every one of them executed with zero ad spend.

#### The zero-ad-spend daily #1 path

One founder took a Product Hunt daily #1 with no ad budget at all (self-reported), using two levers:

- **Launch on Thursday, Friday, or Saturday** — the low-competition days most teams avoid. Fewer heavyweight launches means the same vote count ranks higher.
- **One-by-one LinkedIn community DMs** — personalized, sent individually, never bulk. Slow and manual, and it works precisely because nobody else is willing to do it.

#### The badge-or-relaunch rule

- Only the **top 5** on the daily, weekly, or monthly boards get a badge — everything below that is a launch with no durable asset.
- If launch day goes sideways and top 5 is out of reach, there is an escape hatch: **email PH support to remove the launch**, then relaunch on a quieter day.
- Treat this strictly as a **last resort** — the practice is increasingly policed, and a removed-and-relaunched product that gets flagged loses more than a badge.

#### The core formula

Strip away the tactics and launch marketing reduces to one equation:

> **Marketing = precise user-channel matching × high-quality content design**

The repeatable 4-step SOP behind it:

| Step | Action |
|:---|:---|
| 1 | Fill the positioning template — who is it for, what pain, why now |
| 2 | Turn "channels" into a **named list** — actual subreddits, groups, newsletters, not categories |
| 3 | Build a 3-piece content pack: one-liner + key features + user cases |
| 4 | Reuse **80%** of the pack across channels, customize the remaining **20%** per channel |

#### Launch ROI benchmark (self-reported, consulting engagements)

- A well-run launch typically costs **~$10-20K** end to end.
- Expected return: **hundreds of thousands to ~1M impressions** and **1K-10K+ signups**.
- Minimum viable crew: **1 full-time + 1-2 interns**.
- If your numbers land far below this band, the usual culprit is channel matching (step 2 above), not content quality.

#### The launch-day combo

The highest-leverage single-day stack observed:

- Community posts across your named channel list
- **DM the competitor's followers** — they already have the problem your product solves
- One launch article published the same morning
- Directory submissions batched for launch day

One competitor's zero-budget launch reportedly covered **~40 channels in one day** with this combo.

Distilled from 12 founder podcast interviews (2024-2026), figures self-reported.
