# Korean Speech Samples

This sample shows Korean contributor speech in a consistent audio format. It is meant to help buyers review recording quality, language coverage, and metadata structure before scoping a larger delivery.

## What This Shows

- Korean speech recordings from contributor collection workflows
- Clip-level metadata for format and review context
- Ground-truth transcripts for understanding sample content

## Metadata Coverage

The value is not just the audio clip; it is the combination of listenable media, transcript status, language labels, speaker context, and technical audio metadata that lets a buyer evaluate fit before requesting broader coverage.

Sample metadata includes: sample identifiers, sample titles, language labels, language codes, media filename, format, container, codec, sample rate, channels, file size, speaker format, transcript text, transcript source, and transcript segments.

## Sample Scope

The sample includes 2 clips. Ground-truth transcripts are included for review. Larger deliveries can be scoped by coverage, format, volume, metadata depth, and licensing requirements.
