This is a dataset of simultaneous interpretation policy / trajectory, includeing EN, ZH, JA, KO translation.
Each language has 2,000 records of asr data and 6,000 records of simultaneous interpretation trajectory data.

Source: [amphion/Emilia-Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amphion/Emilia-Dataset)

Please refer to [github](https://github.com/LUOXIAO92/MakeSense) to see the usage
