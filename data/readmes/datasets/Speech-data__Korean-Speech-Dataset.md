# 🎧 Korean Speech Dataset

The **Korean Speech Dataset** is a large-scale **speech audio dataset** designed to provide high-quality and structured **audio data** for advanced AI and machine learning systems. It includes **192 hours of audio data** across **628 files**, delivered in **MP3 and WAV formats**, with a total size of **447 MB**. This well-balanced **audio dataset** ensures diverse and representative **voice data**, with **52% female and 48% male speakers**, and an age distribution ranging from **18 to 50+ years**. The **dataset language** is Korean, with recordings collected from South Korea, North Korea, and global Korean-speaking communities, making it a robust **language speech dataset** for real-world applications.

## 🚀 Use Cases

This **Korean speech dataset** is designed for a wide range of AI applications, including **speech recognition**, voice assistant development, and natural language processing. The structured **speech data** supports acoustic modeling, speaker identification, and scalable training pipelines, making it a reliable **speech recognition dataset** for both research and deployment. It is also well-suited for multilingual systems that require consistent and high-quality **speech audio dataset** inputs across different accents and environments.
