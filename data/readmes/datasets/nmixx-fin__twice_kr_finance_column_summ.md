## FinOpinion-Summ-ko

- A task to verify whether the **summary of a Korean economic column** is correct.
- Labeled as **1** if correct, otherwise **0**.
- Utilizes [**AIHub document summarization text**](https://aihub.or.kr/aihubdata/data/view.do?currMenu=115&topMenu=100&aihubDataSe=realm&dataSetSn=97).
