# LucyESL: what Korean English-teaching job adverts state (monthly record and salary statistics)

Aggregate statistics computed by LucyESL (https://lucyesl.com) from the live English-teaching job adverts on its board in South Korea. Snapshot taken 2026-10-20.

**Files.** `monthly-YYYY-MM.json`: one record per month, written once and never changed. It gives the share of live adverts that state the monthly salary, teaching hours, total on-site hours, severance (퇴직금), national pension, health insurance, airfare, paid vacation and split shifts, with the change on the month before. Source: https://lucyesl.com/monthly. `salaries.json`: medians by region and employer type, effective pay per teaching hour against the statutory minimum wage, disclosure rates and housing (https://lucyesl.com/salaries). `disclosure.json` (https://lucyesl.com/disclosure) and `market.json` (adverts by region, https://lucyesl.com/market) follow the same pattern.

**What this is not.** These are counts of adverts, not of employers. The board includes listings copied from other public job boards (marked "Copied listing" on the site), so they show the advertised market, not wages actually paid. Silence in an advert is not a denial. Every figure carries its denominator. Segments below the published minimum size are withheld.
