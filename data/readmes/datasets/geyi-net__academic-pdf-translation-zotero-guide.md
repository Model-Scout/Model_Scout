## 1. Core Capabilities for Universities, Archives & Patent Teams

- **100% Layout & Formula Preservation**: Multi-column IEEE/Springer/Elsevier papers, LaTeX mathematical equations (`$E=mc^2$`), complex chemical structures, tables, and footnotes remain intact in both side-by-side bilingual PDF and target-language PDF outputs.
- **High-Difficulty Historical & Classical OCR**: Supports vertical Japanese (`縦書き`) and kuzushiji (`くずし字`), Classical Chinese (`古籍豎排 / 繁體文獻`), Hanja (`고문헌 한자`), Arabic/Persian RTL manuscripts (`المخطوطات العربية والفارسية`), and Fraktur/Kurrent German.
- **Native Zotero 6 & Zotero 7 Plugin**: Open-source integration ([**GitHub: zotero-geyi-translator**](https://github.com/Nayuta-9/zotero-geyi-translator)).
