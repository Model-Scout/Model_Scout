## 🆕 What's New (July 2026): The AFFiNE 2022–2023 Campaign, Firsthand

First-hand numbers and the compounding case study behind this playbook, from founder podcast interviews (2024–2026). Recalled figures are marked self-reported.

**The zero-paid-traffic campaign.** AFFiNE started SEO in February 2023 with zero paid acquisition — pure high-quality content. Traffic multiplied ~25x within ~3 months, and by the founder's exit (April 2024) the site held a steady ~400–500K impressions/month, all from SEO (self-reported).

**The compounding keyword: "open source notion alternative".** AFFiNE's single most-invested article overtook AppFlowy for the term and held Google top 3–4. When Notion announced its ban on Russian users, search demand for the term spiked — and that one article automatically absorbed a wave of Russian users, with zero extra work. That is what SEO compounding looks like: one high-authority page keeps paying, and catches event-driven demand spikes on its own.

**Production discipline behind the numbers (2022–2023 playbook):**
- Cadence: ~4 articles/week, ~20/month — the "quality over volume" route.
- Before writing: feed the target keyword to Google, study the current page-1 articles (about half are SEO pieces), distill a ~70% framework from them, then out-write those five on quality and user perspective.
- ~1,500 words per piece, images + GIFs. Bounce rate is a ranking signal: readers leaving within 5–10 seconds hurts; dwell time and internal clicks lift the whole domain.
- Maintain a shared asset library (fresh user cases + competitor screenshots) and refresh the product/competitor shots in top-ranking articles every year.

**⚠️ Two-standard note (read before copying parameters).** The 2022–2023 campaign selected keywords at KD 30–50 / volume ~1,000 — viable for an already-authoritative domain riding a hot category. The operative standard in this playbook for new sites remains **volume 300–1,000 / KD 5–35** (2026-06-24 execution standard). Treat the historical parameters as a reference point for mature, high-authority domains — not a replacement.

### Core Framework: SEO + GEO Dual Engine

```
  Traditional Search (Google/Bing)
  ┌──────────────────────────┐
  │  Keywords + Backlinks    │ ← SEO
  │  Schema + Core Web Vitals│
  └────────────┬─────────────┘
               │
        ┌──────▼──────┐
        │  Structured  │ ← Shared Foundation
        │  Content     │
        └──────┬──────┘
               │
  ┌────────────▼─────────────┐
  │  IndexNow + AI Crawlers  │ ← GEO
  │  Direct Answers + Tables │
  └──────────────────────────┘
  AI Search (ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude)
```

### Five Core Principles

1. **Start from BOFU, work upward** — High-intent keywords first (pricing, comparisons). Keyword filter: **volume 300–1,000 / KD 5–35 / traffic potential 300+** (new site only fights in this range)
2. **Authentic voice = best E-E-A-T** — Founder stories, real numbers, first-hand experience
3. **Structured = Citable** — Key Stats tables, FAQ Schema (FAQPage JSON-LD, 5–8 real user search queries), comparison matrices
4. **IndexNow is GEO infrastructure** — Push to **Bing IndexNow** after every update (lets LLMs discover/index before Google crawlers)
5. **Comparison pages are SEO gold** — One page per competitor, capture decision-stage users
6. **Page optimization specifics (2026-06-24)** — Internal links **≤2–3 per paragraph** (more = spam signal), actively cite competitor blogs as external links (trust signal), title formula **best/free/top/guide+year**, meta description keyword rephrase, BreadcrumbList schema, freshness date visible
7. **Quality backlinks not bulk** — $20–30 outreach to review sites buys 20–30 quality links; forum bulk links (e.g. BacklinkBeam) carry penalty risk
