## FinNewsBQA-CLS-ko
- A **binary QA dataset** built based on [**AIHub news machine reading comprehension data**](https://www.aihub.or.kr/aihubdata/data/view.do?currMenu=115&topMenu=100&dataSetSn=577).  
- Given an **economic news text** and a **query**, the response is either **Yes/No**.
