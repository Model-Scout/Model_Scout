# High School Graduation Exam Scores in Vietnam, 2023

## Dataset Description

- This dataset contains the scores of 1.5 million high school students in Vietnam who took the 2023 National High School Graduation Exam. 

- The comprehensive two-day exam covers six subjects, with mandatory tests in Mathematics, Literature, and Foreign Language. Additionally, students choose one of two subject combinations:

  - Natural Sciences: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  - Social Sciences: History, Geography, Civic Education

- Candidates select their preferred foreign language from the following options: N1 - English; N2 - Russian; N3 - French; N4 - Chinese; N5 - German; N6 - Japanese; N7 - Korean.

## Data Fields

The dataset, in CSV format, includes the following columns:

1. 🆔 `StudentID`: Unique identifier for each student.
2. 🧮 `Mathematics`: Scores in the Mathematics subject.
3. 📚 `Literature`: Scores in the Literature subject.
4. 🌐 `Foreign Language`: Scores in the Foreign Language subject.
5. ⚙️ `Physics`: Scores in the Physics subject.
6. 🧪 `Chemistry`: Scores in the Chemistry subject.
7. 🌱 `Biology`: Scores in the Biology subject.
8. 📜 `History`: Scores in the History subject.
9. 🌍 `Geography`: Scores in the Geography subject.
10. 📖 `Civic Education`: Scores in the Civic Education subject.
11. 🗣️ `Foreign Language Code`: Code representing the chosen foreign language
