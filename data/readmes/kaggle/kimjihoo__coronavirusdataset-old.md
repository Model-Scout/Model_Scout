# Coronavirus-Dataset

Dataset of COVID-19 in South Korea (Old Version)

We have decided to leave the **old version** of Coronavirus-Dataset, because many kernels had been made based on it.
[Link to the new version of Coronavirus-Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/kimjihoo/coronavirusdataset)

## Context
COVID-19 has infected more than 7000 people in South Korea.
KCDC (Korea Centers for Disease Control & Prevention) announces the information of COVID-19 quickly and transparently.
We make a structured dataset based on the report materials of KCDC and local governments.
