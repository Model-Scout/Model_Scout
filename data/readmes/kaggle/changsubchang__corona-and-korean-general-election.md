# Corona and Korean General Election

Detailed dataset on Korean recent elections, to the municipal government level

### Context

As a team project of Graduate School of Data Science, Seoul National University. The Team collected, cleansed, and preprocessed election data of 20th and 21st General Election in Seoul metropolitan area, to find the potential relations with Corona virus spread in Korea


### Content

The contents include:
1. General election data of 20th and 21st General Electtion
2. Corona data of the Seoul Metropolitan Area
(The data is different from readily available data of another Kaggle data, since it includes regional cut all the way down to smallest municipal governments)
