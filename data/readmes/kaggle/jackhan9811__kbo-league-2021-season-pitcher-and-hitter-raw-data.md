# KBO League 2021 season Pitcher and Hitter raw Data

2021시즌 KBO리그 투수 타자 기록

Attached csv file contains raw dataset of 2021 Korean Baseball Organization League (KBO Leauge).

The dataset is comprised of pitcher and hitter data.

No filter (Hitters with a record of at least one Plate Appearance (PA) and pitchers with a record of at least one pitch) 

Datasets were scraped from https://www.statiz.co.kr using R Selenium.

To get access to the R codes used for scraping, please contact me via email.
