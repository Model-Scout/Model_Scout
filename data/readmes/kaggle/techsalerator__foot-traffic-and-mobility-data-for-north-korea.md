### **Top 5 Key Data Fields**
1. **Geospatial Coordinates (Latitude & Longitude)** – Enables precise mapping of mobility events and foot traffic hotspots.  
2. **Foot Traffic Volume** – Measures the number of visitors or pedestrians within a defined location over time.  
3. **Dwell Time & Visit Duration** – Tracks how long individuals stay within a specific area or venue.  
4. **Origin-Destination Movement Patterns** – Analyzes movement flows between locations to understand travel behavior.  
5. **Time-Stamped Activity Data** – Captures mobility trends across hours, days, and seasons for temporal analysis.  

---

### **Top 5 Foot Traffic & Mobility Trends in North Korea**
1. **Urban-Centered Mobility Concentration** – Higher activity levels observed in major cities such as Pyongyang and select administrative hubs.  
2. **Limited but Structured Movement Patterns** – Mobility is often concentrated around workplaces, government zones, and approved public areas.  
3. **Time-Bound Activity Fluctuations** – Foot traffic varies significantly based on work schedules, public events, and regulated movement windows.  
4. **Restricted Inter-Regional Mobility** – Movement between regions is generally controlled, resulting in localized mobility clusters.  
5. **Institutional and Public Venue Activity Dominance** – Foot traffic is largely associated with government facilities, transport hubs, and designated commercial areas.

2. **Humanitarian & Development Analysis** – Supports NGOs and international organizations in understanding population distribution and access to services.  
3. **Transportation & Mobility Optimization** – Assists in analyzing travel flows and identifying key transit corridors.  
4. **Economic Activity Estimation** – Provides indirect indicators of commercial activity and regional economic engagement.
