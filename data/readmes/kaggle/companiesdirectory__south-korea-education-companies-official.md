## Provenance / sources

Records are classified by companies.directory from open location datasets
(Overture Maps via BrightQuery, Foursquare, OpenStreetMap POIs) and, where
available, official registers. Names, categories, cities and online presence
come from those sources; contact details and street addresses are withheld
from this snapshot and live on [companies.directory](https://companies.directory).
