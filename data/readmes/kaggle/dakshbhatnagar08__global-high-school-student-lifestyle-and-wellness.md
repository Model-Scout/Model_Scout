# Global High School Student Lifestyle & Wellness

Simulated data on global high school student habits, stress levels, and AI use.

### This dataset is a **synthetically generated simulation** of **500 high school students** from around the world, designed to reflect common **lifestyle patterns**, **academic habits**, and **wellness indicators** in **2025**.

---

### **Features Included**

- **Demographics**: Age, gender, country, grade  
- **Lifestyle Habits**: Sleep duration, daily screen time  
- **AI Usage**: Whether the student uses AI for studying  
- **Academic Pressure**: Stress level (1–10), board exam preparation status  

---

### **Countries Represented**
**USA, UK, India, Canada, Australia, Germany, Brazil, South Korea, Nigeria, Japan**

---

### **Note**
This dataset is **synthetic**, meaning it was generated using **probabilistic distributions** to simulate realistic data.  
**No personal or survey data** was used.

---

### **Use Cases**

-  **Visualization** & storytelling  
-  **Predictive models** for stress or AI adoption  
-  **Educational research** & behavior analysis  
-  Practice with **classification, clustering, correlation, or regression tasks**
