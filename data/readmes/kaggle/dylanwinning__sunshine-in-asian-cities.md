# Sunshine in Asian Cities

Monthly sunshine in Asian cities

This dataset shows the hours of monthly sunshine for cities in Asia.

The data comes from cities in Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Lebanon, China, Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Iran, Iraq, Japan, Israel, Kazakhstan, Mongolia, Oman, North Korea, Pakistan, Philippines, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Turkey, Uzbekistan, United Arab Emirates, and Vietnam.

Each column provides the hours of **total** sunshine for the month. 

This data is as of May 2023. This data may not be accurate in the future.
