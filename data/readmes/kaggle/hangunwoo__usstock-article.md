# usstock-article-dataset

this is a korean article dataset of '미국 주식 갤러리' in dcinside.

This is a korean article dataset of '미국 주식 갤러리' in dcinside, which is the largest community site in Korea. There is over 60k+ article data, and each article has been evaluated by Gemini 2.5 flash for relevance to US stocks. Also, sentiment analysis by positive / negative / neutral was also performed on articles evaluated as relevant.
