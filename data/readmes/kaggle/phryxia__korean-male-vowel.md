# korean-male-vowel

Tiny Korean vowel phonome records in wav 48KHz 16bit format

Tiny Korean vowel phonome records in wav 48KHz 16bit format. These voice are mine.

- 30 years old Korean male, untrained as voice actor
- Recorded by me using CM7010 usb microphone
- Moderate noise reduction using GoldWave v6.80
- FIR high pass filter above 85Hz were applied
