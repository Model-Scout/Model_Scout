## Policy for Utilizing Public Data
Public institutions ensure that anyone can conveniently access public data through the Public Data Portal and strive to universally expand the scope of usage rights.

## Permissible Range of Public Data Usage
Public data containing third-party rights, such as copyrights, must obtain proper usage permissions from the rightful owners. When providing public data containing works, the appropriate open data license types specified by relevant laws, including copyright laws, should be applied.

[**Reference**] **Public Data Management Guidelines Article 8**
- Article 8 (Management of Public Data Containing Works)
① *The head of a public institution must clearly understand the scope of rights subject to the works and the permissions of the rights holders when managing public data containing works to prevent infringement on third-party rights. In this case, the rights holder's permission must be obtained explicitly through methods like written documents to confirm their intent.*
② *When a public institution provides public data containing works, it must indicate the scope of usage permissions for the public data by attaching the appropriate open data license types specified in relevant laws such as the "Copyright Act." In cases where third-party rights are included in the relevant public data and the rights holder grants permission, the data must be provided to users in a manner that allows them to use the work freely.*
③ *If works included in the relevant public data were created and disclosed as part of official duties or if the public institution possesses all the copyright and related property rights of those works according to contracts, users should be allowed to use the works freely without separate permission, except in cases falling under any of the provisions in Article 24-2(1) of the Copyright Act.*
