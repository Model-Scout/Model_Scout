# Multilingual Audio Book Dataset



The dataset contains information on 82,517 audio books in multiple languages, including English, Hindi, Spanish, German, French, Catalan, Swedish, Italian, Danish, Finnish, Dutch, Hebrew, Polish, Galician, Afrikaans, Icelandic, Romanian, Tamil, Portuguese, Urdu, Hungarian, Czech, Bulgarian, Mandarin Chinese, Basque, Korean, Arabic, Greek, Turkish, Ukrainian, Slovene, Norwegian, Telugu, and Lithuanian.

Each row in the dataset represents a unique audio book, with detailed information such as the title, author, narrator, duration, release date, price, rating, and rating count. The dataset provides a comprehensive overview of the audio book collection, encompassing a wide range of genres and languages.

This diverse audio book dataset can offer valuable insights into language preferences among listeners, the popularity of audio books in different languages, and the distribution of audio book ratings across languages. By analyzing this dataset, audio book platforms and publishers can make data-driven decisions to cater to the diverse linguistic needs and interests of their audience.
