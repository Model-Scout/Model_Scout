# 2016-2025 Kdramas Dataset

From Goblin to Squid Game: 10 Years of Korean Drama Excellence

**Korean Drama Dataset (2016-2025)**

**Overview**
This comprehensive dataset contains information about Korean dramas (K-dramas) released from 2016 to 2025. It includes 285 titles with details about their broadcast years, episode counts, ratings, and availability across major streaming platforms.

**Dataset Contents**

The dataset includes the following columns:
- **title**: Name of the Korean drama
- **release_year**: Year of release (2016-2025)
- **episodes**: Total number of episodes
- **ratings**: Average user rating (on a scale of 1-10)
- **netflix**: Availability on Netflix (yes/no)
- **viu**: Availability on Viu (yes/no)
- **prime_video**: Availability on Amazon Prime Video (yes/no)
- **disney_plus**: Availability on Disney+ (yes/no)

## Key Features

- **Time Span**: 10 years of K-drama releases (2016-2025)
- **Total Entries**: 285 Korean dramas
- **Rating Range**: From 5.9 to 9.3
- **Episode Range**: From 4 to 129 episodes

## Potential Use Cases

This dataset can be used for:
- Analyzing rating trends across different years
- Comparing streaming platform availability
- Studying the relationship between episode length and ratings
- Identifying the highest-rated dramas by year
- Tracking the growth of K-drama production over time
- Analyzing platform distribution patterns

## Notable Insights

The dataset includes highly acclaimed dramas such as "When Life Gives You Tangerines" (9.3 rating), "Twinkling Watermelon" (9.2), "Hospital Playlist 2" (9.1), and "Alchemy of Souls" (9.1), as well as popular titles like "Crash Landing on You," "Goblin," and "Queen of Tears."

## Data Source

This dataset has been compiled from **AsianWiki** and **MyDramaList**, two comprehensive online databases dedicated to Asian entertainment. It provides a detailed overview of the Korean drama landscape from 2016 through 2025, with ratings and information aggregated from these trusted drama communities.
