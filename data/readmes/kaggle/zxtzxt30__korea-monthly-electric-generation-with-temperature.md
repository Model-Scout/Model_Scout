# Korea Monthly Generated Electricity

Korean Monthly Generated Electricity of each province with Temperature

Generally speaking, the generated electricity is not the amount of consumed electricity. There might be several losses during transmission. 

In the circumstance of Korea, the generated electricity of each province is sent throughout Korea using a transmission network.

The column of data means generated electricity in each province. And this data contains information from 2002 to 2020.
