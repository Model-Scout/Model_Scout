# Most Frequent Verbs English Korean Parallel List

1000 Most used verbs in daily conversation

### Context

As I am on my journey to learn Korean and I am trying various strategies. I thought It could be useful to learn the most used verbs in daily life to get a strong basis. I thought it could also help someone else as I have been looking on Kaggle for such datasets.

### Content

There are only two rows for now; English, Korean. The verbs are sorted by daily use frequency. It is a simple parallel list so you can train using this directly. Perhaps if you have ideas to make it more interesting you can share your ideas and we could work on it.


### Acknowledgements

I started by seeking the most used English verbs, the list was from this [website](https://www.educall.com.tr/blog/post/500-most-common-english-verbs).
For the Korean parallel, I made a small python script using the [googletrans](https://py-googletrans.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) library.


### Inspiration

As for now, there is no real data science use case, It is mostly for those who need a list of verbs to focus on for an optimized learning.
