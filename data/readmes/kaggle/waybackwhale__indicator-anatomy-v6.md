# Regional Depopulation Indicator Anatomy v6

Classroom data for SQL-based regional depopulation indicator analysis

Four compact Parquet tables, a simplified sigungu boundary GeoJSON, and a Korean font used in University of Seoul classroom notebooks on regional depopulation indicators. The package supports reconstruction of the simple risk index, an educational eight-component composite score, annual comparisons, official 89-area matching, capital-region youth migration analysis, and simple choropleth maps. The composite score is an educational reconstruction from public proxy data, not the government's unpublished regional raw score.
