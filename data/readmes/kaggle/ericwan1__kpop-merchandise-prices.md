# KPop Online Merchandise Prices

Prices of K-Pop Goodies from JYP, SM Entertainment, Musicplaza, and more!

This dataset contains prices of k-pop albums, posters, photobooks, and other merchandise from multiple online e-commerce platforms. These sites include the official fan shops for JYP and SM Entertainment, as well as third party vendors with locations in the USA such as Musicplaza, Kpopalbums, and Kpopstoreinusa. Lastly, Mwave is included in this dataset, which while based in South Korea, is one of the only places to reliably procure autographed merchandise.
