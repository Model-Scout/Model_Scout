# webtoon_originals_modifiedversion

Webtoon dataset comprising artist origins (Korean or non Korean)

This dataset is a modified version of an existing dataset on Kaggle. I manually added a column in Excel indicating whether a webtoon is Korean or non-Korean (0 for Korean, 1 for non-Korean). The information regarding the origin of the webtoon artists was researched online by myself and may contain inaccuracies due to human error.
