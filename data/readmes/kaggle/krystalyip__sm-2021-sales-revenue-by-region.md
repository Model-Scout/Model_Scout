# SM 2021 Sales revenue by region



In 2021, SM Entertainment derived around 521.6 billion South Korean won in sales revenue from its homeland South Korea, while a further roughly 75.5 billion on of sales revenue was generated in Japan. SM Entertainment is one of the biggest South Korean entertainment companies and manages several major artists such as Super Junior, SHINee, EXO, Red Velvet, NCT, and aespa. Based on music production and management of K-pop artists, its business areas expanded to video content production, travel, and advertising.
