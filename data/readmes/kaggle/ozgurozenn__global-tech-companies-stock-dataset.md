# global-tech-companies-stock-dataset

This data was retrieved from yfinance.

This dataset provides detailed financial and company information for over 40 leading technology companies from around the world. It includes major players in semiconductors (NVIDIA, Intel, TSMC), consumer electronics (Apple, Samsung, Sony), computer hardware (Dell, HP, Lenovo), and more.

**Key Features:**
- Current stock prices and historical data (52-week high/low)
- Market capitalization values
- Company details 
- Industry and sector classifications
- Geographic diversity (US, Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, China, Hong Kong)

**Ideal for:**
- Stock market analysis
- Technology sector research
- Portfolio optimization
- Financial modeling and predictions
- Comparative company analysis
