# 33 Car Image Classification



**33 Car Models Image Dataset**
- This dataset is composed of 33 different car models currently merchandising in South Korea. 
- Hyundai Motor, Kia Motor, Genesis Motor, Ssangyong Motor, Renault korea Motors
- Data was collected by selenium from google image. 
- You can classify images by Convolutional Neural Networks.
