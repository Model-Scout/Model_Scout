# North Korean nuclear research

Information on nuclear-related studies published by North Korean scientists

The dataset covering titles and authors of studies on nuclear issues published by the Journal of Kim Il Sung University between 1991 and 2017.

- DPRKtech.csv: Information on titles and authors of the studies published between 1991 and 2017.
- Keywords.csv: List of words that possibly indicate risks of nuclear proliferation

There could be some typos which will be revised and updated periodically. 

Special thanks to Dr. Justin Hastings of the University of Sydney. A significant part of the dataset was created by Dr. Hastings, and the data collection project was funded/sponsored by his research project.
