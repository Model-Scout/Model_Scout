# Ultimate Korean Drama List

Dataset with over 400 popular kdramas of all times.

This dataset feature the most popular korean dramas of all times and some related factors.

##### **Context**
This file contains data of all 406 dramas listed in the Dataset. The dataset consists of movies released on or before 2022. Data points include Drama Name, Year of Release, Watch Time, Drama Rating, Genre, Votes and Actors.

**Possible uses for this dataset could include:**
- Sentiment analysis in a variety of forms
- Categorising Dramas based on their comments and statistics.
- Analysing what factors affect how popular a drama will be.
