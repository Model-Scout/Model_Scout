# PMPL South Asia 2020 (PUBG)

The Data contains results of each match of Pro League PUBG Mobile

**Context**
PlayerUnknown's Battlegrounds (PUBG) is an online multiplayer battle royale game developed and published by PUBG Corporation, a subsidiary of South Korean video game company Bluehole. It is a very popular game worldwide and its craze among users is increasing day by day. 
In the game, up to one hundred players parachute onto an island and scavenge for weapons and equipment to kill others while avoiding getting killed themselves. The available safe area of the game's map decreases in size over time, directing surviving players into tighter areas to force encounters. The last player or team standing wins the round.

In PMPL players play in a group (team) of 4 players, a total of 16 teams play in a match i.e. 64 players play at a time. If all players of a team are dead then that team gets eliminated from that particular match and other teams continue to fight for 1st position. The Team that is alive till the end of the match (at least one player of that team must be alive) wins the i.e. get Winner Winner Chicken Dinner. Teams get points placement points according to when they got eliminated from the match.
For instance, the team eliminated at position 16 means it was the first team to get eliminated from that match.

**Content**
PUBG organizes tournaments all over the world. Teams participate in those tournaments and top x teams are promoted to next round all the way to enter into World League. PUBG Mobile South Asia Pro League is a PUBG Mobile professional league for the South Asia region. The league features 20 teams from India & South Asia. The Split will culminate at the PUBG Mobile World Championship 2020.
This data contains details of every match of PMPL South Asia like placement points, kill points, total points, chicken dinner, map, etc.
The goal is to see how teams are performing in every group, map, and overall and draw beautiful insights through visualizations.
