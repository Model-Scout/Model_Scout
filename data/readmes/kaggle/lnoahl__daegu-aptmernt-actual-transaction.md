# daegu apartment actual transaction

Public apartment sales records in Daegu, South Korea

This dataset contains **actual real estate transaction records** for apartments in **Daegu, South Korea**.
It is sourced from the official **Actual Transaction Price Disclosure System** operated by the Korean government, covering nearly **20 years** of transaction history (2005–2024).
The dataset is ideal for housing price prediction, long-term market trend analysis, and urban development research.
It can also serve as a regional counterpart to widely used real estate datasets, enabling comparative modeling and experimentation.

**Column Description**

* ID: unique identifier for each transaction
* city: city name (e.g., Daegu Metropolitan City)
* county\_or\_gu: district or county name
* sub\_locality: sub-administrative unit (dong, eup, myeon, ri)
* address\_number: lot number (beonji)
* main\_number: main lot number
* sub\_number: sub lot number
* road\_name: road name address
* building\_name: name of the apartment complex or building
* building\_block: building block or unit designation
* floor: floor number
* exclusive\_area\_m2: exclusive residential area in square meters
* total\_area\_m2: total floor area in square meters
* land\_area\_m2: land area in square meters
* land\_ownership\_area\_m2: land ownership area in square meters
* construction\_year: year of building construction
* contract\_year\_month: contract year and month (YYYYMM)
* contract\_day: contract day (DD)
* housing\_type: housing type (e.g., apartment, officetel, etc.)
* transaction\_type: type of transaction (intermediary or direct)
* transaction\_price\_krw\_10k: transaction price in units of 10,000 KRW
