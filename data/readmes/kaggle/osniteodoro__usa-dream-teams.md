# USA Men's Basketball Dream Teams

A pergunta que não quer calar: Qual foi o melhor Dream Team?

The curiosity is that Barcelona-1992 marked the first Olympic edition in which NBA professionals were allowed to play. With them on the court, the United States regained Olympic hegemony in men's basketball, which was completely dominated by the country from the debut in Berlin-1936 to Mexico City-1968 with seven gold medals won undefeated.
The hegemony fell in Munich-1972, when the Soviet Union imposed on the United States the first defeat in the final of the Olympic Games by 51 to 50. The Americans wanted a rematch in Montreal-1976, but the Soviets were defeated in the semifinal and the victory in decision was against Yugoslavia.
Because of the boycotts, there was no new meeting between Americans and Soviets in Moscow-1980 and Los Angeles-1984. In 1987, a new blow to the self-esteem of the United States happened with the defeat by Brazil in the Pan American Games of Indianapolis-1987. The following year, in Seoul-1988, the elimination to the Soviet Union in the semifinal left the bronze medal won with an extremely bitter taste.
The failures increased lobbying for the release of NBA professionals, who were officially allowed to play in FIBA-sponsored tournaments in 1989 and created the opportunity for the 1992 Team to be created.
