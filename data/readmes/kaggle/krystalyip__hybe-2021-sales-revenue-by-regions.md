# HYBE 2021 SALES REVENUE (BY regions) 



In 2021, HYBE Corporation, formerly known as Big Hit Entertainment, derived around 360.3 billion South Korean won in revenue from online activities. The majority of revenue generated that year came from outside South Korea, which accounted for around 214.3 billion won in revenue. HYBE is one of the biggest South Korean entertainment companies and owns several music labels, with major artists such as BTS, SEVENTEEN, and TXT under their care.
