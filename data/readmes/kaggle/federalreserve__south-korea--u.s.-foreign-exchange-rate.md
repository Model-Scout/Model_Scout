### Context  

This is a dataset from the [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov) hosted by the Federal Reserve Economic Database (FRED). FRED has a data platform found [here](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) and they update their information according to the frequency that the data updates. Explore the Federal Reserve using Kaggle and all of the data sources available through the Federal Reserve [organization page](https://www.kaggle.com/federal-reserve)!  

* Update Frequency: This dataset is updated daily.
