# Kpop song rankings

Ranking of kpop songs since 2010

Weekly ranking of 200 most listenned kpop songs since 2010. Scrapped on circlechart.kr which is the korean official ranking system of kpop songs. 

The data is present in one csv file with the following columns :
- year : Year of the ranking (2010 to 2023)
- time : Week of the year of the ranking (1 to 53)
- rank : Ranking of the song (1 to 200)
- song_title : Title of the song
- artist : Artists of the song, can be multiple and separated by a comma
- album : Album of the song
