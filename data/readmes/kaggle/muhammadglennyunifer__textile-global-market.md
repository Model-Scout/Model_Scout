# Textile Global Market

A cross-country dataset for Exploring Indonesian New Textile Export Destinations

This dataset contains information on 148 countries worldwide that can serve as potential new destinations for Indonesian textile exports. Ten countries with the largest textile import values from Indonesia in 2023—namely the United States, Japan, China, South Korea, Germany, Netherlands, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, and Belgium—are excluded, as they already account for 71.44% of Indonesia’s total textile export value (COMTRADE, 2025).

The dataset provides three main dimensions for exploration:

Trade Indicators: average textile import growth (2020–2023), average textile trade balance (2020–2023), and average global market share of textile imports (2020–2023).

Economic Potential: population size, purchasing power parity (PPP per capita, USD), and share of industry in GDP.

Trade Barriers: logistics performance index (LPI), geographic distance from Indonesia (in kilometers), and status of free trade agreements (FTA) with Indonesia.

This dataset can be used for trade policy analysis, market selection studies, export development strategies, and economic research focusing on international trade and market access.
