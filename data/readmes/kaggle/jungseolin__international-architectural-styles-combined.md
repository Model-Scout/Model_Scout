# International Architectural Styles - Combined

For those who yearned for a less Eurocentric architectural styles dataset.

**International Architectural Styles - Combined** is an extended version of my original dataset, which you can find [here](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jungseolin/international-architectural-styles).

It contains 45 international architectural styles from around the world. The latter 25 styles are a combination of the works of 

- Danci, Marian Dumitru (**@dumitrux**) 
	     [link to his dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dumitrux/architectural-styles-dataset)

- Xu, Zhe & Zhang, Ya & Tao, Dacheng & Wu, Junjie & Tsoi, Ah. (2014). Architectural Style Classification Using Multinomial Latent Logistic Regression. 10.1007/978-3-319-10590-1_39. 
	    [link to their dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wwymak/architecture-dataset)

My contribution to the dataset is composed entirely of images from **Wikimedia Commons**. For those 20 styles, each folder includes an `attributes.txt` file with the title, creator, source, and copyright information of each image with the header number corresponding to the label of the image under style/data.
