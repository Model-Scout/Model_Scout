#  Booking.com hotel review

Republic of korea hotel review

### Abstract

I crawled with team members (Shin Mi-yong, Kim Jae-hyun, Park So-hyun) and booking.com hotel reviews for the project.


### About this dataset + How to use 

this datasets is korea  region: Yeosu, Jeju Island, Seoul, Gangneung, Busan, Sokcho, Gyeongju, Gwangju, Incheon, Gunsan, Chuncheon, Gapyeong, etc  36669 hotel reviews.

Most of them crawled over three-star hotels.
Guest House, Resort Inc. - Busan, Jeju Island, Jeju Island
It can be used as data for the hotel review-based system!
