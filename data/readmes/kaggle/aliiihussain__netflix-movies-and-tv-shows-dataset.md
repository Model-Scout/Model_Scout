# Netflix Movies and TV Shows Dataset

Dataset of global movies and TV shows for research and training.

📌 **Overview of the Dataset**

The Netflix Movies & TV Shows – Elite Synthetic Dataset is a premium, high-fidelity collection of 1,200 simulated entries modeled after real-world streaming platform data. It captures the structure, style, and diversity of modern entertainment catalogs while remaining safe, synthetic, and fully customizable.

📁 **1. Dataset Structure**

This dataset includes 12 core fields, each representing a commonly used streaming metadata element.

**Key Columns**

- **show_id** – Unique identifier for each movie or TV show
- **type** – “Movie” or “TV Show”
- **title** – Synthetic content title
- **director** – Fake director crafted from a limited set
- **cast** – A list of fictional actors
- **country** – Country of production
- **date_added** – Date the show/movie was added to the platform
- **release_year** – Original release year
- **rating** – Age/maturity rating
- **duration** – Minutes (for movies) or seasons (for series)
- **listed_in** – Genre or category
- **description **– Short content description

🎬 **2. Content Types**

**Movies**
- Duration given in minutes
- Single-entry format
- Covers all genres: Drama, Action, Sci-Fi, Horror, Comedy, etc.
 **TV Shows**
- Duration expressed as seasons
- Represents serialized entertainment
- Includes shows from various countries and styles

🌍 **3. Geographic Diversity**

The dataset incorporates content from different parts of the world including:

- United States
- United Kingdom
- Canada
- India
- Japan
- South Korea
- This helps simulate global streaming patterns found on real platforms.

🔍 **4. Metadata Quality & Realism**

Even though the dataset is synthetic, it is designed to feel authentic and high-quality.

**Realistic Features**
- Balanced distribution of genres
- Mixed release years from 1950–2024
- Varied age ratings
- Diversity in directors and actors
- Dates that resemble real streaming upload patterns
This allows for:
- Trend analysis
- Recommendation system training
- Machine learning experiments
- Data visualization

✨ **5. Strengths of This Dataset**
- Completely synthetic (no copyright issues)
- Safe for commercial and academic usage
- High realism for practical modeling
- Balanced metadata
- More than 1,200 entries—large enough for real analysis

📝 **6. Summary**

This dataset provides a premium, polished simulation of the kind of metadata used by global streaming platforms. With realistic attributes and diverse content, it serves as an excellent foundation for learning, experimentation, and professional projects.
