# Collider Detection using Vibration data 

Collider Detection AI in Nuclear Plant | Competition by DACON

### Context

Vibration data to predict the x-coordinate, y-coordinate, mass, and velocity of the collider

Competition Organizer/Host
- Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute
- DACON

[See competition site](https://dacon.io/competitions/official/235614/overview/)

### Problem description

Colliders that exist inside the coolant system of a nuclear power plant can cause damage to equipment. 
At the nuclear power plant site, we are trying to prevent accidents by diagnosing abnormal signs of equipment early. 
The purpose of this competition is to activate artificial intelligence technology research that can diagnose abnormal signs of devices early.
