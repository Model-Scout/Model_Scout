# JIT_dataset

Jejueo Interview Transcripts

### Context

Jejueo, or the Jeju language, is a minority language used on Jeju Island. It was classified as critically endangered by UNESCO in 2010. While there have been many academic efforts to preserve the language data-driven approaches for Jejueo-related language tasks have been rare. Motivated by this, we developed the Jejueo Interview Transcripts (JIT) dataset, a Jejueo-Korean parallel corpus.

### Content

The entire sentence pairs, 170,356, are split into 160,356, 5,000, and 5000 for train, dev, and test, respectively.
