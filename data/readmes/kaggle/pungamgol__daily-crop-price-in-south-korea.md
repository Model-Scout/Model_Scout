# Daily crop price in South Korea



Daily data on crop prices were obtained from the Garak market of the Seoul Korea Agriculture Fisheries & Food Trade Corporation (aT). Except for radish, data from 2010 to 2023 were collected and all data were obtained from the KAMIS homepage (https://www.kamis.or.kr/customer/price/agricultureRetail/item.do).
