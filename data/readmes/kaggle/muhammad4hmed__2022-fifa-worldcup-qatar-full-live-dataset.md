# 2022 FIFA Worldcup Qatar
### Updated every week!
## Context
The 2022 FIFA World Cup is scheduled to be the 22nd running of the FIFA World Cup competition, It is scheduled to take place in Qatar from 20 November to 18 December 2022. This will be the first World Cup ever to be held in the Arab world and the second World Cup held entirely in Asia after the 2002 tournament in South Korea and Japan. The first match played at the tournament will be contested between Qatar and Ecuador at Al Bayt Stadium, Al Khor. The final is due to be held on 18 December 2022, which also is Qatar National Day. The reigning World Cup champions are France.
