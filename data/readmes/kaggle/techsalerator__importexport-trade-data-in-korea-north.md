**Top Trade Trends in North Korea**

- **Sanctions Impact:** North Korea's trade activities are significantly influenced by international sanctions, which affect both its import and export operations. These restrictions impact the scale and scope of trade transactions.
- **Limited Trade Partners:** North Korea primarily conducts trade with a few key partners, notably China and Russia. This limited network affects the diversity and volume of trade.
- **Focus on Strategic Goods:** Trade often focuses on strategic goods and resources, including coal, textiles, and military equipment. These commodities are central to North Korea's trade strategy and economic activities.
- **Smuggling and Informal Trade:** Due to sanctions, there is a notable presence of smuggling and informal trade activities, which impact the official trade data and its accuracy.
- **Economic Isolation:** The country’s economic isolation from the global market affects its trade dynamics, leading to unique challenges and opportunities within its trade sector.
