**Key Data Fields**

- **Job Posting Date:** Captures the date a job is listed. This helps job seekers and HR professionals stay current on available opportunities and trends.
- **Job Title:** Specifies the advertised position, which assists in categorizing and filtering job openings by industry roles and career interests.
- **Company Name:** Lists the hiring entity. This information helps job seekers target their applications and enables businesses to track competitors and market trends.
- **Job Location:** Provides the job's geographic location within North Korea. Job seekers use this to find regional opportunities, while employers analyze regional talent and market conditions.
- **Job Description:** Includes details about responsibilities, required qualifications, and other relevant aspects. This is essential for candidates to assess their fit for the role and for recruiters to communicate expectations clearly.
