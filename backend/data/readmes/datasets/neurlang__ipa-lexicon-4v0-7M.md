# IPA Phonetic Lexicon (6.9M words)

- This is an International Phonetic Language lexicon containing 6.9 million utterances across 350+ languages.
- All speech files were converted into IPA using our [neurlang/ipa-whisper-medium](https://huggingface.co/neurlang/ipa-whisper-medium) model.
- Postprocessing to join multiword IPA into single-word IPA record for single-word headwords was performed for appropriate languages.

## ⚠️ Limitations
- Speaker distribution is highly uneven across countries
- Some languages are overrepresented (e.g. English, Spanish)
- Country labels/languages may be inacurrate for some users (e.g. Antartica)
- Audio quality varies by contributor
- IPA generation is model-derived (not manually verified)
- Regional dialect distinctions may be blurred
