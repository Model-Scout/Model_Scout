# multilingual_single_sentences

This dataset consists of single-sentence completions spanning a diverse range of languages, including Russian, German, Spanish, Korean, Chinese, English, French, and Japanese. The content varies widely, covering topics from historical restoration and travel logistics to proverbs and daily observations. Each entry is presented as an isolated text completion without additional context or paired prompts.

### Dataset size

There are 10,274 data points in this dataset. This is an instruction tuning dataset.

### Quality of Remastered Dataset

The final quality is A, with a relative quality improvement of 55.0%.
