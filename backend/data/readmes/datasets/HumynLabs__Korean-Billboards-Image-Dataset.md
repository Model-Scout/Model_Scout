# Korean Billboards Image Dataset
*This dataset contains high-quality images of Korean billboards, collected from diverse regions and media types including digital boards, street hoardings, transit ads, and building-mounted displays. It supports AI research in OCR, advertising analytics, and multimodal vision-language understanding.*

## Supported Tasks

- **Task Categories**:  
  - Image Classification  
  - Text Recognition (OCR)  

- **Supported Tasks**:  
  - Text detection and transcription in billboard advertisements  
  - Visual classification of ad types (digital, static, transit, etc.)  
  - Brand logo and product recognition  
  - Multimodal analysis of text + imagery in marketing visuals  
  - AI research in advertising placement and visual saliency detection  

## Languages

- **Primary Language**: Korean  
- **Secondary Presence**: English (often in brand names or slogans)  

## Dataset Creation

### Curation Rationale
This dataset was curated to help develop AI systems capable of understanding commercial and advertising content in public spaces. It focuses on billboard text recognition, branding pattern detection, and visual content categorization, contributing to research in marketing AI and urban computer vision.

### Source Data
- **Contributors**: Field photographers and volunteer data collectors across South Korea  
- **Collection Process**: Photos were captured from public spaces including roadsides, metro stations, and commercial districts. Each image was reviewed to remove any personal identifiers or sensitive data before inclusion.  

### Other Known Limitations
- **Lighting Conditions**: Some images may include low-light or night-time captures with reflections or shadows  
- **Bias**: Urban areas (Seoul, Busan, Incheon) are more represented than rural regions  
- **Content Variance**: Focused mainly on static and semi-digital billboards; limited representation of video-based or dynamic LED ads  

## Intended Uses

### ✅ Direct Use
- Training OCR systems for Korean advertising contexts  
- Studying multimodal text-image relationships in marketing visuals  
- Brand recognition and trend analysis for research purposes  
- Cultural studies of advertising language and imagery  

### ❌ Out-of-Scope Use
- Commercial re-use of actual ad content without proper rights  
- Surveillance or location tracking using billboard backgrounds
