## What is this?

A battle-tested community building playbook for developer tools and SaaS products. Real frameworks validated through Notion, Lovable ($50M ARR via community-led growth), and AFFiNE (0→60K GitHub stars in 24 months).

Covers the full community lifecycle: from zero to first 100 members, through ambassador programs, Discord/Slack channel architecture, community-led growth flywheels, event operations, KOC (Key Opinion Consumers) activation, and health metric tracking.

## What's Inside

- **Community Strategy** — CLG (Community-Led Growth) flywheel design, positioning community as a product growth lever
- **Discord & Slack Setup** — Channel architecture, onboarding flows, moderation SOPs for developer communities
- **Ambassador Programs** — Recruitment, tiering, incentive design, and retention frameworks
- **Community-Led Growth (CLG)** — From 0 to first 1000 members, UGC activation, KOC programs
- **Event Operations** — Online/offline event playbooks, hackathons, AMAs, community calls
- **Health Metrics & KPIs** — DAU/MAU ratios, activation rates, contribution funnels, churn signals
- **Real Case Studies** — Notion community flywheel, Lovable $50M ARR CLG strategy, AFFiNE 60K stars OSS community
- **Templates & Benchmarks** — Ready-to-use onboarding messages, ambassador briefs, community health dashboards
