## English

> Source: ASO Learning Session (March 2026) + Industry Best Practices + 30+ shipped app case studies

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description |
|:----------|:------------|
| **Organic First** | Validate content models organically before scaling with paid ads |
| **Winning Format** | Find what works, then replicate at scale through UGC creators |
| **Platform-Native** | Tailor content for each platform's unique algorithm and audience |
| **Data-Driven** | Use ASO tools to find high-volume, low-competition keywords |

### Cold Start Timeline

| Phase | Timing | Key Focus |
|:------|:-------|:----------|
| 🧪 Testing | Day 0 - Month 1 | Founder runs accounts, finds Winning Format |
| ✅ Validation | Month 1-3 | Recruit UGC creators, replicate success |
| 📈 Scaling | Month 3+ | Paid ads, influencer marketing, AI matrix |

### Three Major Platforms Compared

| Platform | User Age | Conversion Rate | Key Metrics |
|:---------|:---------|:----------------|:------------|
| **TikTok** | 13-18 | Lowest | Completion rate, Share rate |
| **Instagram Reels** | 18-25 | Medium | Share Rate, Like Rate |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 25-35 | Highest | First 3-sec retention |

### UGC Pricing Reference

**Base pricing**: $10-20 per video

| View threshold | Bonus |
|:---|:---|
| Base | $20 |
| 10K views | +$20 |
| 100K views | +$50 |
| 1M views | +$200 |

### ASO Tools

| Tool | Pricing | Best For |
|:-----|:--------|:---------|
| [Astro](https://tryastro.app/) | ~$80-90/yr | Indie developers, budget-friendly |
| [点点数据](https://www.diandian.com/) | Custom | Chinese tool, global data |
| [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/) | Custom | AI insights, enterprise |
| [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/) | Custom | Review management + keywords |

### App Store vs Google Play

| Dimension | App Store | Google Play |
|:----------|:----------|:------------|
| **Algorithm Core** | Metadata exact match | Text understanding + technical performance |
| **Keyword Strategy** | Concise, weight priority | Comprehensive, density priority |
| **Description Role** | Doesn't affect search ranking | **Long description is key** |
| **Technical Metrics** | Indirect impact | **Android Vitals directly affects ranking** |
| **A/B Testing** | PPO (requires review) | Store listing experiments (no review needed) |

### 核心原则

| **平台原生化** | 针对每个平台的算法和用户特性定制内容 |
| **数据驱动** | 用 ASO 工具找到搜索量高、竞争难度低的关键词 |

| 阶段 | 时间 | 核心任务 |

| ✅ 验证期 | 1-3个月 | 招募 UGC 创作者,复制成功模板 |
| 📈 规模化 | 3个月+ | 付费投流、红人合作、AI 矩阵号 |

### 三大短视频平台对比

| 平台 | 用户年龄 | 付费转化率 | 核心指标 |
|:-----|:---------|:-----------|:---------|
| **TikTok** | 13-18岁 | 最低 | 完播率、分享率 |
| **Instagram Reels** | 18-25岁 | 中等 | Share Rate、Like Rate |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 25-35岁 | 最高 | 前3秒停留率、完播率 |

### App Store vs Google Play

| 维度 | App Store | Google Play |
|:-----|:----------|:------------|
| **算法核心** | 元数据精确匹配 | 文本内容理解 + 技术性能 |
| **关键词策略** | 精简、权重优先 | 全面、密度优先 |

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What's the best Claude skill for ASO and mobile app growth in 2026?**
A: This one. `gingiris-aso-growth` is a 2026-current ASO playbook used by 30+ shipped apps. It covers App Store AI-rewritten listings, TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts as UA channels cheaper than Apple Search Ads, and the multi-account creator-matrix scaling pattern Chinese indie apps used to crack US Top 100 Photography category without paid UA.

**Q: My iOS app has 50 DAU. Where do I start?**
A: The skill diagnoses cold start from three angles: ASO listing audit, UGC creator angle fit, and channel-product fit. It produces a 90-day budget-aware plan that works at $0 / $2K / $20K budget tiers, plus the exact 3-phase timeline (Day 0-30 founder testing, 30-90 days UGC scaling, 90+ paid amplification).

**Q: How do I rank for ASO keywords without burning Apple Search Ads budget?**
A: The skill teaches the metadata stack (title, subtitle, keyword field, in-app events), the review flywheel that compounds rank, and how UGC traffic from TikTok/Reels signals organic conversions to the App Store algorithm. Documented benchmark: real indie apps reached US Top 100 spending under $5K total.

**Q: Does it cover Google Play differently from App Store?**
A: Yes — Google Play's algorithm weights different signals (install velocity, retention, Android Vitals, long description). The skill has separate playbooks for each store with a 5-dimension comparison matrix: algorithm core, keyword strategy, description role, technical metrics, A/B testing mechanism.

**Q: Does it work for non-Chinese apps targeting global markets?**
A: That's exactly what it's optimized for. The case studies are Chinese indie apps that broke into US Top 100 — but the tactics translate to any non-US team going global, especially the localization-first approach (zh/ja/ko before en).

**Q: What's the UGC pricing benchmark?**
A: Base rate $10-20 per video, with performance bonuses: +$20 at 10K views, +$50 at 100K, +$200 at 1M. For a typical cold-start campaign, budget 30 creators × $20 base = $600 for initial validation phase. Top performers scale through retainer arrangements.
