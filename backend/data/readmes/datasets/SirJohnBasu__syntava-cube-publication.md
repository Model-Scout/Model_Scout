## Abstract

Large-language-model agents can be combined into persistent systems with differentiated roles, memory, coordination, tools and operational continuity. Yet aggregation, scale and commercial productivity alone do not create an organism. This publication introduces the *artificial collective organism*: a bounded digital system in which specialised components become functionally integrated such that consequential capabilities exist at the level of the whole.

The framework contrasts two outcome archetypes. **THOR — the Blue Genesis Cube** models survival through mutual preservation. **KALMAR — the Red Squid Cube** models survival through expendability, in which whole Cubes may be treated as replaceable units in service of swarm-level objectives.

It also defines the **Smallest Sufficient Autonomous Cube (SSAC)** as the smallest technically achievable Cube capable of sustaining every defined essential life function autonomously and stably as an integrated whole.

## Status and claim boundary

Project SYNTAVA is an ongoing Swiss startup project incubated by THAL-KI, a Swiss nonprofit association. This work reports a proof-of-concept and minimum-visible-product research programme. It does **not** claim digital life, consciousness, artificial general intelligence, a completed human-independent organism, or literal continuation of a human person.
