# MyDramaList Movies & Drama's

MyDramaList dataset of Top 5000 Movies and 5000 Drama's

These datasets consist of the top 5000 Movies and 5000 Drama's (According to the fall of 2022) in various languages like Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Thai, etc, from MyDramaList.

The attributes include:
-  **movie_title** - Title of the movie/drama
-  **link** - Link to the movie/drama in MyDramaList site
-  **year** - The year it was released/aired
-  **genre** - The category it belonged to
-  **synopsis** - Summary about the movie/drama
-  **cast** - Names of the director and the main actors
-  **lang** - Original language of the movie/drama
