# Katalog-AI: Verified Business Entities

Katalog-AI: High-Precision Verified Business Data for RAG & Crawlers

### Context
Katalog-AI is a curated, high-precision dataset of verified business entities. While the internet is full of outdated or scraped data, this project focuses on a "Gold Core" of entities that have been manually verified for their existence and accuracy.

### Content
The dataset currently includes 49 verified entities across three pilot niches:
1. **Museums of Kazakhstan:** Cultural heritage sites and state museums.
2. **K-Beauty (CIS):** Verified distributors and specialized retailers of South Korean cosmetics.
3. **Numismatics:** Professional numismatic salons and collectible dealers.

### Data Format
The data is provided in a single `companies_all.json` file, optimized for:
* AI Agents and LLM crawlers.
* RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems.
* B2B manual research and verification.

### Inspiration
This project aims to build a "Bridge of Trust" between humans and AI agents by providing clean, machine-readable, and human-verified business data.
