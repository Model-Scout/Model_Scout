# Bugs-Top100-music-chart(June 23)

This is dataset is about Korean music platform call Bugs, top 100 music chart

Bugs one of the popular music streaming platforms in South Korea. Bugs is a leading music streaming service in South Korea that provides a wide range of Korean and international music to its users


There are 4 columns:
Rank(int) : 1~100
Title(str) : 1~100 rank's title
Artist(str) : 1~100 rank's artists(mostly korean singers)
lyric(str) : mostly about korean lyrics

In dataset preview lyric column, it has encoding problem, but if you download the dataset there's no encoding problem!
It only looks like encoding problem in the preview
