# OECD Better Life Index

Latest Better Life Index from OECD

There is more to life than the cold numbers of GDP and economic statistics – This Index allows you to compare well-being across countries, based on 11 topics the OECD has identified as essential, in the areas of material living conditions and quality of life.

FAQ: https://www.oecdbetterlifeindex.org/about/better-life-initiative/

**Data source(s) used**

European Union Statistics on Income and Living Conditions (EU-SILC), National Statistical Offices.

**Data characteristics**

Percentage of the population
The value is an average of data available between 2015-20. Data refer to 2020 for Belgium and Mexico, 2018 for the United Kingdom, 2015 for Korea, 2014 for the Russian Federation, 2010 for Brazil, 2008 for Japan, 2001 for Chile and 1997 for Canada.
