# 2026 FIFA World Cup — Historical Elo Ratings

125 years of Elo ratings for all 48 qualified teams (1901–2026)

See README.md on this page for the full dataset description, column dictionary, methodology, provenance, predecessor bridging notes, and suggested uses.

Quick facts:
- 4,683 rows, 23 columns, one row per (team, year-end snapshot)
- 48 qualified teams for the 2026 FIFA World Cup
- Coverage 1901–2026, plus a live-snapshot row per team
- Federation lineage bridged for Czechia, Germany, Curaçao, DR Congo, Egypt, and South Korea
- Confederation, host, and 2026 exit-round columns included

Source: World Football Elo Ratings (eloratings.net). License: CC BY-SA 4.0.
