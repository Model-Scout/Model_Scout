# Spotify Top 50 Playlist Songs | @anxods

Spotify Top 50 Playlist Songs (World, USA, UK, Spain, Mexico and others...)

Are you interested in knowing how's your favorite song performing in **Spotify's Top 50** Songs Chart Playlists? Then take a further look into this dataset.

We take daily, at around 09:15 UTC, the data from the following Top 50 Songs Playlist countries:

- United States 🇺🇸
- Spain 🇪🇸
- United Kingdom 🇬🇧
- Italy 🇮🇹
- France 🇫🇷
- Mexico 🇲🇽
- Argentina 🇦🇷
- Japan 🇯🇵
- South Korea 🇰🇷

This data is retrieved by the Spotify API and then collected into one individual CSV file per country.
