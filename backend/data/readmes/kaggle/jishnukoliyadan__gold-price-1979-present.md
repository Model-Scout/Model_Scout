# Global Gold price - Historical Data (1979-Present)

Daily, Monthly and Quarterly Gold rates of major Consumer and Producer counteris

### About Gold Rates

Gold price discovery is crucial for any market. Gold not only has a spot price, but it also has the LBMA Gold Price, as well as several regional prices. The LBMA Gold Price is used as an important benchmark throughout the gold market, while the other regional gold prices are important to local markets.  This is a Time Series data.

![Gold](https://i.imgur.com/OMtJxZ7.jpg)
### Whats in this dataset ?

- The gold rate from 1979 to present 2022-03-11 was includes.
- The rates are calculated in 1 troy ounce, ie in 31.1034768 grams
- 3 `csv` files for representing Daily, Monthly and Quarterly gold prices,
1. `Daily_Gold_Price_on_World.csv`
1. `Monthly_Gold_Price_on_World.csv`
1. `Quarterly_Gold_Price_on_World.csv`
