### Content
The dataset consists of in total 2901 ranked matches from Korea(WWW), North America(NA), Eastern Europe(EUNE), and Western Europe(EUW) servers. It has which team won the match, the total time of the match, blue team composition and red team composition. Note that only the high elo games were added this includes Challenger, Grand Master, Master and sometimes even High Diamonds. Note that there are 153 total unique champions with 'Rell' as the latest addition. Duplicate games have been removed.
