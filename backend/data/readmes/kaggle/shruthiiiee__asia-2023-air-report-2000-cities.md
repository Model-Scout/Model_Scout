# Asia's 2023 Air Report (2000+ Cities)

Tracking Air Quality in Asia's Cities

![](https://www.googleapis.com/download/storage/v1/b/kaggle-user-content/o/inbox%2F14439374%2F3790a838621cd161cf7556c6da3f9626%2Fdownload%20(1).jpg?generation=1711526063585377&alt=media)

This dataset provides comprehensive information on the air pollution index of over 2164 cities across Asia for the year 2023. It covers countries including Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, Georgia, Hong Kong SAR, India, Indonesia, Iraq, Israel, Japan, Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, Laos, Macao SAR, Malaysia, Maldives, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan, Palestinian Territory, Philippines, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Singapore, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Tajikistan, Thailand, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, Uzbekistan, and Vietnam.

The dataset includes the ranking of cities based on their annual average PM2.5 concentration (μg/m³). 

Each entry contains details such as the rank of the city, city name, average pollution index for the year 2023, as well as monthly pollution levels from January to December. Additionally, it provides the corresponding country for each city.

&gt;- **Rank:** The ranking of cities based on their annual average PM2.5 concentration (μg/m³).
- **City:** The name of the city.
- **2023:** The average pollution index for the year 2023.
- **Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec:** Monthly pollution levels from January to December.
- **Country:** The corresponding country for each city.

&gt;### Potential Use Cases:
1. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Understand the distribution of air pollution levels across Asian cities and identify potential trends and patterns.

2. **Predictive Modeling:** Forecast future air pollution levels based on historical data to assist in planning and decision-making for environmental management.

3. **Geospatial Analysis:** Analyze the spatial distribution of air pollution across Asia to identify hotspots and prioritize areas for intervention and policy implementation.
