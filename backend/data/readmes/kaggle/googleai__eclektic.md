# ECLeKTic

Evaluate Cross-Lingual Knowledge Transfer with this multilingual QA dataset

This is ECLeKTic, a multilingual closed-book QA dataset that Evaluates Cross-Lingual Knowledge Transfer in a simple, black-box manner.

The questions in this dataset were generated based on Wikipedia articles in 12 languages (English, German, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Portuguese and Spanish) selected under the condition of not having equivalent articles in the other languages. The result is a dataset targeting facts that are well-known in their source language but not in the other languages. A model capable of answering correctly the questions in ECLeKTic is a model that is capable of abstracting knowledge from the language in which it was acquired.

The details of the dataset creation and results of some SOTA models are in [in our paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.21228).
