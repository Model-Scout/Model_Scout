# Multilingual Depression Detection Dataset



![banner](https://proteus-prod-public.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/temp/8ac35f51-edb2-4859-9107-361ceeb9336b.png)

This dataset contains text samples in Arabic and Korean expressing severe depression, trauma, and suicidal ideation. Each entry consists of a prompt asking whether the text indicates mental health issues, paired with a binary label confirming the presence of such conditions. The content is designed for training models to detect critical mental health signals in multilingual social media or forum posts.
