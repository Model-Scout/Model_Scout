# multilingual_mental_health_prompts



![banner](https://proteus-prod-public.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/temp/0af34dee-9e2e-4993-b535-955d3d09ec27.png)

This dataset contains multilingual text samples responding to a standard prompt about mental health feelings and issues. The entries span diverse languages including English, Spanish, Arabic, Urdu, and Korean, featuring personal expressions of depression, anxiety, and emotional distress. The content varies from brief statements to detailed personal narratives, often including social media formatting like hashtags and URLs.
