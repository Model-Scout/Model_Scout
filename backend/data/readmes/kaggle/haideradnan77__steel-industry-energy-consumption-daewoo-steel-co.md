# Steel Industry Energy Consumption: DAEWOO Steel Co

A Year of High-Resolution Energy Consumption Analysis at DAEWOO Steel.

# **Description**
```python

Energy efficiency is the backbone of modern heavy industry. This dataset provides a detailed record of electricity consumption from DAEWOO Steel Co. Ltd in Gwangyang, South Korea. As a major producer of steel coils, steel plates, and iron plates, the facility's energy footprint is massive and highly dynamic.

The data was collected via a cloud-based system and synchronized with the Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO). In the context of InduCognix AI, this dataset serves as a benchmark for developing "Energy-Aware" industrial agents that can predict demand peaks and optimize machine scheduling to reduce operational costs and carbon footprints.
```
