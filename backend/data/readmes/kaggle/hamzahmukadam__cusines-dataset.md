# Cusines dataset

Dataset contains images of foods from different 10 different cuisines

The dataset contains images of dishes from 10 different cuisines and has a total of 400 images. The cuisines are: Italian: Pizza, Pasta, Risotto, Lasagna, Tiramisu
Mexican: Tacos, Burritos, Enchiladas, Guacamole, Salsa
Chinese: Dumplings, Stir-Fry, Kung Pao Chicken, Fried Rice, Egg Rolls
Japanese: Sushi, Ramen, Tempura, Yakitori, Matcha Ice Cream
Indian: Curry, Naan, Biryani, Samosas, Lassi
American: Hamburgers, Hot Dogs, Fried Chicken, Apple Pie, Mac, and Cheese
French: Croissants, Quiche, Coq au Vin, Ratatouille, Crème Brûlée
Thai: Pad Thai, Green Curry, Tom Yum Soup, Mango Sticky Rice, Som Tum Salad
Greek: Gyro, Souvlaki, Moussaka, Feta Cheese, Baklava
Korean: Kimchi, Bibimbap, Bulgogi, Jjajangmyeon, Tteokbokki
