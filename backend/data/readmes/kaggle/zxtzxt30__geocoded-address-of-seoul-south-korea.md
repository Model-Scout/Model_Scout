# Geocoded address of Seoul, South Korea 

Longitute & Latitute of address in Seoul; Capital of Korea

These are the longitude and the latitude of the address in Seoul, South Korea.

This data contains almost entire communal buildings in Seoul above 200 households and the data on secure spaces or buildings is excepted.

It is really useful to visualize data on a map.

Lon : Longitude
Lat : Latitude
Addr : Address of communal building in Seoul
