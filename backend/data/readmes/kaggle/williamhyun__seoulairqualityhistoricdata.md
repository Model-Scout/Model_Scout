# Seoul Air Quality Historic Data (1988 ~ 2021)

Air quality metrics (SO2, NO2, CO, O3, PM10, PM2.5) with latitude and longitude

This dataset contains air quality historic data in Seoul Metropolitan area.

- The period of data: 34 years (1988 ~ 2021)
- The interval of collection: hourly
- The number of records: 5,984,783
- The number of location: 25
- The number of air quality metrics: 6

Data Source:
- https://data.seoul.go.kr/
