# Instagram Captions of K-Pop Idols with Their MBTI

You can use this dataset to predict MBTI of K-Pop Idols

What I'm trying to achive by collecting this data is that I want to predict the MBTI of K-Pop idols based on their Instagram captions. I collected the captions using instaloader. This dataset contains 458 idols from various groups, I gathered the MBTI and Instagram username of idols from kprofiles.com and kpopmap.com. The captions collected are only until November 2022.
