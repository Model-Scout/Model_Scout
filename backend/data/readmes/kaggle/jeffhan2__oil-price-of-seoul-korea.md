# Price of Oil in Seoul, Korea

Uncovering Seoul's Oil Prices

**Seoul Gas Station Dataset**

This dataset contains information about gas stations in Seoul, providing valuable insights into the city's fueling infrastructure. The dataset encompasses various details such as station names, locations, fuel types offered, and additional services provided.

**Attributes:**
1. **Station Name:** The unique identifier for each gas station.
2. **Location:** Geographical coordinates (latitude, longitude) indicating the station's position.
3. **Fuel Types:** Information on the types of fuels available at each station, including gasoline, diesel, and LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas).
4. **Additional Services:** Any supplementary services offered by the gas stations, such as car wash, convenience store, or vehicle maintenance.

**Use Case:**
This dataset is valuable for anyone interested in analyzing or visualizing the distribution of gas stations across Seoul. It can aid in making informed decisions related to fuel accessibility, market trends, or potential areas for future gas station development.

Feel free to explore and utilize this dataset for your analytical and research endeavors related to Seoul's fueling infrastructure!
