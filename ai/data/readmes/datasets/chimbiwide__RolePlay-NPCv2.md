# RolePlay-NPCv2

The newest RP dataset containing some high-quality dataset for Gemma3NPC.

---

We combined [pippa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chimbiwide/pippa), [NPC-Dialogue_v2](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chimbiwide/NPC-Dialogue_v2), [Sonnet-Roleplay](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chimbiwide/Sonnet-RolePlay) and [ReLe_Synthetic_v1_json](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chimbiwide/ReLe_Synthetic_v1_json).
