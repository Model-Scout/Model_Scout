# Korean Conversational Speech Dataset

500 hours of spontaneous two-person conversations in Korean, recorded in South Korea with 50 vetted native speakers. Dual-channel audio, time-aligned human-verified transcripts and speaker metadata, licensed for commercial ASR, TTS and voice AI training.

**This repository is the dataset card only.** The audio is licensed commercially and isn't hosted on Hugging Face. Free samples (audio, matching transcripts and the metadata schema) are sent on request: [request Korean samples](https://www.speechdata.ai/datasets/korean?utm_source=huggingface&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=dataset_card). ## What makes Korean hard for speech recognition

Korean sounds change at word boundaries in ways the Hangul spelling doesn't show, and particles and endings attach to words, so word forms multiply. Speech levels shift between formal and casual depending on who's talking, and conversation is full of English loanwords.
