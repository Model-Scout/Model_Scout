# Foundation_AI

### Train your LLM in the way it should go, and when it is old it will not depart from it.
We offer foundation layer training data in 101 languages* to be used BEFORE pretraining.

The foundation layer approach aims to achieve four objectives:
1) Improve training efficiency and accelerate convergence
2) Reinforce positive and non-discriminatory behavior
3) Mitigate hallucinations and harmful suggestions
4) Enhance potential for cross-lingual generalization
