# snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0-ko ONNX (int8) for Transformers.js
ONNX export of [dragonkue/snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0-ko](https://huggingface.co/dragonkue/snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0-ko) (Apache-2.0) with dynamic int8 quantization, laid out for Transformers.js (`onnx/model_quantized.onnx`, dtype `q8`). CLS pooling + L2 normalization are applied by the caller; queries use the `query: ` prefix.
