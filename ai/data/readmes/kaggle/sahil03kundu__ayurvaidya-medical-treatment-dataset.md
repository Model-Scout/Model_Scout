# AyurVaidya Medical Treatment Dataset



## Overview
100,000 synthetic patient records comparing treatment effectiveness across 10 medical systems: Allopathic, Ayurveda, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Unani, Siddha, Kampo, Korean Traditional Medicine, African Traditional Medicine, Naturopathy, and Integrative Medicine.

## What's Inside
- **100,000 records** across 52 diseases in 14 clinical categories
- **121 features** including demographics, vitals, sensor readings, regional factors, and treatment outcomes
- **84-dimensional engineered feature vector** across 8 feature groups
- **10-class treatment recommendation** labels with cure probabilities and outcomes for each system

## Key Features
- Patient demographics (age, gender, BMI, blood group)
- Ayurvedic constitution (Prakriti) types
- 14 simulated IoT sensor readings
- Regional health zone modulation (WHO regions)
- Knowledge graph embeddings
- Treatment costs, recovery times, side effects, and outcomes for both allopathic and traditional medicine

## Use Cases
- Multi-class treatment recommendation
- Comparative effectiveness research
- Traditional vs modern medicine analysis
- Health equity and regional health modeling
- Multilingual medical NLP (102 languages represented)

## Files
- `medical_treatment_dataset_v3.csv` — Full dataset (100K rows)
- `sample_data.csv` — 200-row sample for quick exploration
