# Bibigo brand reviews and ratings dataset

BIBIGO BRAND MARKET ANALYSIS (INDIA)

This project analyzes CJ CheilJedang's Bibigo brand performance and opportunities in the Indian market. Bibigo, known globally for authentic Korean cuisine including dumplings (mandu), ready-to-eat meals, sauces, and frozen foods, is expanding its presence in India's growing ready-to-eat and K-food market. This analysis aims to provide actionable insights for Bibigo's expansion strategy in India, focusing on market opportunities, consumer preferences, and growth potential in the world's fastest-growing major economy.
