# Korean Sign Language(KSL) Numbers

Dataset of KSL numbers 1 to 10

### Context

This dataset demonstrates the sign language numbers based on the Korean Sign Language(KSL) from numbers 1 to 10.


### Content

This dataset consists of a total of 1100 images from 11 classes, 100 images per number from 1 to 10. Number 10 has two versions available in Korean sign language(KSL) numbers. This dataset is highly useful in real-world applications, where Korean sign language datasets are rarely available.
