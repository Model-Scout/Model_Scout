### Top 5 Technology Trends in North Korea
- **State-Approved Intranet Services**: Due to restrictions on internet access, North Korean organizations rely on Kwangmyong, the government-controlled intranet. Tech solutions that support this system are crucial for communication and operations.

- **Domestic Software Solutions**: With limited access to global software, North Korea has developed its own technology ecosystem. The rise of domestic software products, particularly for education and governmental purposes, is shaping the country’s IT landscape.

- **Automation in Manufacturing**: North Korea is increasingly adopting automation technologies in its manufacturing sector, especially for industries like textiles and heavy machinery, as part of efforts to modernize production processes.

- **Renewable Energy Initiatives**: Due to chronic energy shortages, North Korea is investing in renewable energy technologies, such as solar power, to sustain essential services and industries.

- **Cybersecurity and Encryption**: Given the closed nature of the country, cybersecurity measures are highly prioritized. Organizations rely on government-approved encryption and data protection protocols to secure communications within the national network.
