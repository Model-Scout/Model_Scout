# cafe_delivery_sales

2020-21 data on cafe delivery sales and corona status. ( 카페 배달 매출 데이터 )

Sales data for about one year for delivery sales (delivery app in south korea). (January to November 2021)

Because the structure and appearance of hall sales and delivery sales are very different, only delivery sales are counted, and a higher-level analysis can be performed by comparing the corona status data and weather data.

배달 매출(배달의 민족)에 대한 약 1년간의 매출 데이터입니다. (2021년 1월 ~ 11월) 

홀 매출과 배달 매출의 구조와 모습이 많이 다르기때문에 배달 매출만 집계하였으며, 코로나 현황 데이터와 날씨 데이터와의 비교를 통해서 보다 고차원적으로 분석할 수 있습니다.

(A) Sales amount

   Baemin delivery tip is included in the sales amount for Baedal Minjok service order.

   Baemin 1 service order includes a small order fee.

   The sales amount excludes customer discount costs and may differ from the total payment amount in the order history.

   Order number: This is a unique number for the customer's order.

   Sales classification: This is a large category of payment methods and consists of card sales, cash sales, and other sales (point payment, various payments).

   Payment method: As a detailed category of payment methods, various payment methods such as cash, credit card, Baemin Pay, and Kakao Pay are provided in detail.

   Supply Price: The supply amount of the order received from the customer.

   VAT: VAT on the order amount (10%)


(B) Weather data: It consists of the average temperature (°C), the minimum temperature (°C), daily precipitation (mm) and average wind speed (m/s) in the area (Seoul) for one year.

(C) Corona status data: It consists of the number of tests, the number of confirmed cases, the number of negative results, and the number of cures during the period in Korea.
