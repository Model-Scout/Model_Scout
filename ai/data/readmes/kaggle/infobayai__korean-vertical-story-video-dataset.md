# Korean Vertical Storyline Video Dataset

Large-scale Korean Vertical Storyline Video Dataset for AI training

**Dataset Description:**

This dataset is a **large-scale collection of vertical storyline video content**, designed to support the development of advanced AI systems for short-form video understanding, multimodal learning, storytelling analysis, and next-generation content intelligence.

The dataset contains diverse vertical-format videos optimized for mobile-first platforms, including narrative-driven clips, cinematic sequences, creator content, educational storytelling, lifestyle content, tutorials, and user-generated media.

Additionally, this dataset can be integrated into pipelines for **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) workflows**, helping improve AI performance in video comprehension, contextual reasoning, narrative understanding, recommendation systems, and multimodal generation tasks.


**Key Use Cases**

Short-form video understanding
Storyline and scene transition analysis
Video captioning and summarization
Multimodal recommendation systems
Speech-to-text and subtitle generation
