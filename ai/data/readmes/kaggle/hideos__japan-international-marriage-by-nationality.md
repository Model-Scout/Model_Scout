# Japan International Marriage: By Nationality

Marriages by nationality of couple in Japan (1965–present)

This dataset provides a long-term time series of marriages in Japan broken down by the nationalities of both spouses, covering annual records from 1965 to the present. It captures trends in international marriages, including combinations such as Japanese husband with foreign wife, and foreign husband with Japanese wife, across 19 nationality pairs covering Korea, China, the Philippines, Thailand, the United States, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Peru, and others.

The dataset is formatted in both tidy long and wide formats for easy use in time-series analysis, visualization, and demographic research.

Data is sourced from Japan's Vital Statistics (人口動態調査) via the e-Stat API, provided by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare.
