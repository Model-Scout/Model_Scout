# Survey on Ethnic Homogeneity among Korean Students

2021 Student Survey on Perceptions of Koreanness and Ethnic Homogeneity in Korea

The students provided their responses to the following three questions on a Likert scale.
&gt;Q1. Acceptance of various ethnic groups undermines national cohesion.

&gt;Q2. Pride in ethnic homogeneity characterizes the nation.

&gt;Q3. An ethnically homogeneous nation enhances national competitiveness.
