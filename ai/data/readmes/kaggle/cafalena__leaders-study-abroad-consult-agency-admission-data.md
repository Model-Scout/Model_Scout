# Leaders Study Abroad Consult Agency Admission Data

Admissions for students advised by Leaders Uhak

This dataset is based on graduate school admission success stories published by Leaders Uhak, a South Korean study abroad consulting company, on their website (https://leadersuhak.com/AdmissonPosting). The original data, provided in PDF format from 2006 to 2025, has been converted into an Excel file and subsequently uploaded to Kaggle to facilitate easier access and analysis for a broader audience. This resource will be valuable for students preparing for overseas study and researchers interested in identifying admission trends based on various factors such as major, institution, degree type, and test scores.
