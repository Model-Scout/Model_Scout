# README
I will be updating the dataset again once the tournament ends on the expected date May 29, 2022. The current dataset has only records of games until May 28, 2022. Stay tuned.

# Content
The dataset contains all the games played by Faker during the MSI 2022 tournament, also known as the 2022 Mid-Season Invitational, held on May 5, 2022 with the expected end date on May 29, 2022. Some relevant data include the champions Faker has played, his match performance as showcased by his kills, deaths, assists, and creep scores earned, his opposing team in every match, and many more.

# About Faker
Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, is a League of Legends esports player, currently a mid laner and part owner at T1 (his team name). He has been revered as the face of the League of Legends esports scene, or the Michael Jordan of League of Legends for his impressive feats and countless achievements in this scene.

- Country of Birth: South Korea
- Birthday: May 7, 1996 (age 26)
- Role: Mid Laner

# Collection Methodology
All the data is extracted from the Liquipedia official website. Afterward, the query of data was transferred to Excel spreadsheets where data manipulation and cleansing was conducted such as renaming columns, dropping unwanted columns, and converting variables to the appropriate format.
