# Korean Synthetic Industry News

9,980 fictional Korean economic news articles, LLM-generated

# ⚠️ Every article here is FICTIONAL

Generated by a language model. These events never happened. **Not** real reporting —
do not present, index, or consume any of it as actual news.

9,980 synthetic Korean economic news articles from **gpt-oss:20b** via Ollama.
Each is seeded from a controlled combination of industry, news category, direction,
region, and reasoning difficulty, so the generation metadata works as **weak
supervision** for industry and sentiment classification. Taxonomy is Korean-market
specific: **10 sectors / 80 industries**.

Balanced by construction: all 80 industries appear as a seed
140–144 times each; difficulty splits
3,488/2,996/2,499 easy/medium/hard; direction is
near 50:50 (3,765 positive vs 3,707 negative); and ~10%
(997) are deliberately economy-irrelevant so a model can learn to abstain.

## Files

`train.jsonl` 8,984 · `validation.jsonl` 498 · `test.jsonl` 498
— stratified on difficulty × seed sector, one JSON object per line.

Also included: `industry_taxonomy.json` (label space), `generate_news.py` +
`ollama_client.py` (generator with the exact prompts), `DATASET_SPEC.md` (pipeline
spec), and **`README.md` — the full dataset card, far more detail than fits here.**

## Record fields

`id` · `title` · `body` · `synthetic` (always true) · `meta`

`meta` holds `news_category`, `difficulty` (easy = industry named directly, medium =
one value-chain hop, hard = 2+ hops or opposite effects per industry, none =
irrelevant), `direction` (호재 positive / 악재 negative / 혼합 mixed / 무관 irrelevant),
`seed_industries`, `region`, `generator`, and `mixed_pos`/`mixed_neg` when mixed.
Body length 67–428 chars, median 198.

gpt-oss:20b(Ollama)로 생성한 한국어 경제 뉴스 **9,980건**. 업종·뉴스유형·방향·
