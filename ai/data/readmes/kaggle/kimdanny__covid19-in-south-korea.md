# 2019 Novel Coronavirus cases in South Korea (대한민국)

with SARS(2003), MERS(2015) and population data for comparison

## Context

On December 2019, the first case of Novel Coronavirus was identified in Wuhan, Hubei, China. As of mid-February 2020, the number of confirmed cases in South Korea started to surge exponentially.
