# Fixed enforcement camera of Seoul, South Korea

camera for illegal parking

### Context

There are many enforcement camera in Seoul, South Korea. 
This is location data of illegal parking enforcement camera which is called "CCTV" in Korea.

### Content

- fixed_cctv_for_parking_enforcement.csv
  - Location of camera (latitude, longitude)
  - Korean address, name is included.
  - This is published by Seoul city and I got this from http://data.seoul.go.kr/ and edied some of it. 
  - License: CCL(Creative Commons License)-BY

- district_of_seoul.csv
  - Basic information of district.
  - This is published by Seoul city and I got this from http://data.seoul.go.kr/ and edied some of it. 
  - License: CCL(Creative Commons License)-BY

- seoul-map.json
  - Geodata of Seoul
  - https://github.com/southkorea/seoul-maps
  - License: Apache v2.0
