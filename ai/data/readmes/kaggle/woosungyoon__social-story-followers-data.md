# social_story_followers_data

Benchmarks for developing social perception and intelligence

This dataset was created as an exploratory effort to investigate how image sequences can be incorporated into Theory of Mind (ToM) benchmarks, and what forms of benchmark design and dataset composition such integration might enable. 

The dataset was developed through iterative scenario prototyping, experimental testing, and repeated refinement. Its primary purpose is to support the exploration of new dataset design possibilities for multimodal reasoning evaluation. The visual materials were generated by enhancing the author’s original story ideas and hand-drawn sketches with AI, and then converting them into an animation-style format.
