# Global Automobile: Financial Fundamentals & Stocks

Hourly OHLCV Market Data and Quarterly Financial Fundamentals for 11 Global Auto

## Context
This dataset provides a multi-dimensional view of the global automobile industry, spanning from 2024 to mid-2026. It includes high-frequency stock market data (OHLCV) and deep-dive financial fundamentals (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).

## Content
The data is organized into two primary layers:
1. **Market Layer (OHLCV):** Hourly stock data for tickers like TM (Toyota), HMC (Honda), STLA (Stellantis), and M&M.NS (Mahindra).
2. **Fundamental Layer:** Quarterly financial reports covering revenue, debt, and cash flow positions.

## Tickers Included
- **Japan:** TM, HMC, NSANY
- **India:** MARUTI.NS, M&M.NS, HYUNDAI.NS
- **Germany/Europe:** VWAGY, MBGAF, STLA
- **Korea:** 000270.KS, 005380.KS

## Data Quality Notes
- **Currency:** Values are in local exchange currencies (INR, KRW, USD, EUR).
- **Timezone:** All timestamps are standardized to UTC.
- **Missing Values:** Fundamental data contains NaNs where specific line items were not reported by the company for that quarter.
