# Energy Consumption Dataset: Hanyang University Hospital

This dataset contains high-resolution energy consumption data collected from Hanyang University Hospital in Seoul, South Korea. It is designed to support research in building energy analysis and the development of deep learning models for energy demand forecasting.

### Research Purpose
The dataset reflects its intended use for building energy analysis and developing sophisticated deep learning architectures. By combining the 15-minute interval energy records with the provided meteorological data, researchers can perform various tasks such as load forecasting, anomaly detection, and energy efficiency studies in healthcare facilities.
