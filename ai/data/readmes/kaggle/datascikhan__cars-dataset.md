## 🔗 Realistic Relationships Engineered

The dataset is built on **statistically sound relationships** — not random noise:

- ✅ **Vehicle age** ↔ mileage ↔ maintenance ↔ repairs
- ✅ **Engine size** ↔ power ↔ fuel consumption ↔ CO2
- ✅ **Vehicle weight** ↔ acceleration ↔ fuel economy
- ✅ **Brand premium** ↔ price ↔ luxury features
- ✅ **Condition** ↔ accidents ↔ resale value
- ✅ **Depreciation** ↔ age ↔ mileage ↔ brand
- ✅ **Market demand** ↔ days on market ↔ views
- ✅ **Location** ↔ fuel price ↔ demand ↔ insurance
- ✅ **Warranty** ↔ age ↔ manufacturer policy
- ✅ **Loan payment** ↔ amount ↔ rate ↔ term (mathematically correct)

**No perfectly deterministic relationships** — realistic noise added throughout.
