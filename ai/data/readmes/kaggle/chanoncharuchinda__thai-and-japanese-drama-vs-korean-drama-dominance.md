#### What is MyDramaList.com
According to mydramalist.com, "MyDramaList.com is a community-based project which provides Asian drama & movie fans". On the website, fans can "create their very own drama watchlists, rate dramas and films, write reviews" and many more engaging activities. This dataset is different from the previous one as there are more collection of data on Korean drama, reviews, and actors.
